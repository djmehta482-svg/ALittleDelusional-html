A Little Delusional ✨

Living in a Little Dream I Made Myself

Maybe the Universe Is Secretly on My Side

collecting little moments that feel like magic
soft mornings, pretty thoughts & unrealistic dreams
somewhere between reality and my imagination

I like to believe that life is a collection of tiny, beautiful moments waiting to be noticed. A warm cup of coffee, sunlight falling through the window, a song that feels like a memory, or a random moment that makes my heart smile. Maybe I romanticize everything a little too much, but honestly, why should I stop?

I am learning to dream without asking whether my dreams are realistic. Maybe I am a little delusional, but perhaps every beautiful life begins as an impossible thought. So I will keep believing, keep dreaming, and keep making ordinary days feel a little more magical.
